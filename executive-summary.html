Executive summary

Verification policy (key rules)

  • Every table row links to its official MICHELIN Guide detail page.
  • If a value cannot be confirmed from an official source, it is left blank and flagged unverified. Nothing was inferred.
  • Cost for 2 adults: MICHELIN does not publish dollar totals; left blank/unverified.
  • YouTube/TikTok official links: none found on official sources; left blank/unverified.
  • Instagram verified for 28 of 29 starred restaurants. Commis (chef's personal account only) and Madcap San Anselmo (not searched) remain unverified.
  • Some hours/phones (Commis, Saison, Sons & Daughters, Birdsong, Nisei, Sun Moon Studio, Restaurant Naides) were not fully captured on official pages and are flagged unverified.

Irregularities flagged

  • Niku Steakhouse temporarily closed Mar–May 2025 after a fire; reopened per restaurant announcement (hours listed as normal, flagged).
  • Conflicting dates on secondary sources (some pages dated 2026 vs ceremony June 2025); only guide.michelin.com used.
  • Hotel addresses/distances and nearby-restaurant counts are unverified; ranking is a neighbourhood proxy.
  • Listed-only (no star) details not verified from official detail pages.

Bottom line

The starred-restaurant dataset (29 entries) is fully sourced from the official MICHELIN Guide and ready to publish. The listed-only set, hotels ranking, and exact dollar prices require further verification before they should be presented as fact.
