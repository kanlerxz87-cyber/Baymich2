Methodology

Evidence standards

  • Michelin status: verify on the current official Michelin Guide page; record exact designation and check date.
  • Hours, menu, booking and exceptions: restaurant-owned official pages or dated official notices.
  • Hotel rates: hotel-owned booking channel, with dates, occupancy, room type, currency and fees/tax basis.
  • Activities: operator's official page for schedule, access and closures.
  • Social/video: official profile linked from an official venue/operator page. Creator content is inspiration, not evidence.
  • Costs for two: reproducible arithmetic from official menu/reservation prices. Unknown tax, tip, drinks or fees are disclosed; otherwise the total remains blank and flagged.
  • Unknown facts: remain blank and receive an “unverified” flag; never infer from missing information.

Full research plan and unresolved assumptions: read the methodology and decision checklist. Source links will be attached to each published record.
