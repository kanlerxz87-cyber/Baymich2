DECISION CHECKLIST

What we need to know

These questions remain open based on the verified information currently collected. Each can materially change the recommended hotels, clusters and costs.

5 decision gates
01

What counts as “Bay Area”?

Current working boundary includes San Francisco, East Bay, Peninsula, Marin and North Bay / wine country; it excludes Monterey County and Sacramento. Is that the intended geography?

WHY IT MATTERS · Restaurant and hotel set
02

How close is “around” a hotel?

No walk, drive or transit radius is set. Should the ranking use 3 miles walking, 30 minutes by car, transit time, or a mix?

WHY IT MATTERS · Cluster score and map
03

Which trip dates and budget?

Hotel rates, restaurant menus, availability, taxes and seasonal hours vary. Without dates, a verified trip total or hotel price comparison is not possible.

WHY IT MATTERS · True total cost for two
04

Which recognition belongs in scope?

The current data covers starred restaurants only. Should the guide also list MICHELIN Selected, Bib Gourmand, Green Star and special awards as separate statuses?

WHY IT MATTERS · Completeness and value
05

What does “dinner for two” include?

Should estimates include tasting menus, service charges, tax, drinks, optional pairings and booking fees? These items are not consistently available from official sources yet.

PROPOSED NEXT STEPS · NOT IMPLEMENTED

Suggestions to improve decisions

Review the options before choosing a path. This project does not make assumptions on your behalf; no user input is required to browse the current verified baseline.

HIGH IMPACT

Define a repeatable hotel access score

Count current starred, Bib Gourmand and selected restaurants inside a chosen walk/drive radius; report counts by award type and include only geocoded official addresses. Publish the radius and data date next to every score.

S1
HIGH IMPACT

Use a date-specific two-person cost model

Standardize a meal format (for example, base tasting menu for two), then show menu subtotal, mandatory service charges, tax status and optional pairing separately. For hotels, compare the same dates and cancellation policy.

S2
VERIFICATION

Expand beyond star holders

Import the official current MICHELIN Selected and Bib Gourmand listings as distinct statuses. Keep the star roster as its own filter; never label a recommendation “starred.”

S3
USABILITY

Add verified social and itinerary links

For each venue or activity, link only profiles linked from its official site or otherwise clearly authenticated. Add a last-checked date; leave dead, ambiguous or unofficial accounts blank.

S4
MAP

Publish a true map after address verification

Verify venue addresses from official venue pages, geocode them from a trusted map provider, then offer city clusters and individual markers with status, source and a directions link.
