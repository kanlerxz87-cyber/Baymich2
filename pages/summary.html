Geographic insight

Michelin stars cluster in San Francisco (the densest concentration) and in Napa/Sonoma wine country. The Peninsula, South Bay, East Bay, and Marin each have a handful. Staying in Healdsburg, Rutherford/St. Helena, or central San Francisco puts you closest to the most starred restaurants (see Hotels page).

Pricing

The MICHELIN price symbol ($–$$$$) is shown for every restaurant, taken directly from its official MICHELIN page. Estimated two-adult totals are derived only from that symbol and labelled "(est.)". Exact menu prices were not confirmed for most restaurants and are left blank/unverified rather than invented.

Data integrity

Every table row links to its official MICHELIN Guide page. Any value we could not confirm from an official source is left blank and flagged unverified. No values were inferred or fabricated.
