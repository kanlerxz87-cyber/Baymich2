Questions & missing information

These are the items that must be answered before any decision (where to stay, where to eat, how to spend a budget) can be reasonably made from the data we already have — and the data we do not have.

A. Questions we cannot answer yet (missing information)

Q1. What is the exact total price for 2 adults at each restaurant?
Answer: Not known for most entries. We only have the MICHELIN price symbol ($–$$$$) and a labelled estimate. Exact menu/tasting-menu prices must be confirmed from each restaurant's official site.
Q2. Are the opening hours accurate and current?
Answer: Partially. Hours come from official MICHELIN pages where published; several entries are blank/flagged unverified and must be re-confirmed (holidays, seasonal closures, private events).
Q3. What are each restaurant's Instagram / YouTube / TikTok links?
Answer: None are confirmed yet. Every row is blank/"n/a" for social and needs a search of each official website.
Q4. Is every listed restaurant still open and still starred in 2026?
Answer: Based on the official 2026 selection yes, but star status changes yearly (e.g., 2025→2026 dropped several one-stars). Each entry should be re-verified before guests rely on it.
Q5. How far is each hotel from its nearby starred restaurants (miles / drive time)?
Answer: Not measured. The Hotels page uses qualitative "cluster density," not verified distances.
Q6. What is the complete Bib Gourmand and "listed-only" set?
Answer: Not included in this version. The page covers 39 starred entries only; the full 2026 Bib Gourmand (~117 statewide) and broader recommended list are missing.
Q7. Are hotel nightly rates current?
Answer: No. Reported ranges (e.g., Montage, Madrona, Stanly Ranch) come from third-party reporting and must be checked against official hotel sites.

B. Information we DO have (verified)

Known: Full 2026 starred list (3★ / 2★ / 1★), addresses, cuisine, MICHELIN price symbol, official websites, and hours where MICHELIN publishes them.
Source: MICHELIN Guide California 2026 and each restaurant's official MICHELIN page.
Known: MICHELIN Key hotels (SingleThread Inn, Auberge du Soleil, Meadowood, etc.) and their regions.
Source: MICHELIN Guide Hotels / sftravel.com / Sonoma Magazine.

C. Suggestions to discover missing information

  • Visit each restaurant's official website (linked from its MICHELIN page) for exact pricing, current hours, and social-media icons.
  • Call the restaurant (phone listed on MICHELIN page) to confirm tasting-menu price for 2 adults and holiday closures.
  • Search each restaurant name + Instagram / TikTok / YouTube on the social platforms themselves to verify official accounts.
  • Re-pull the MICHELIN Guide California selection annually (stars change every year) and mark any delisted restaurant.
  • Use a mapping tool with each restaurant's address to compute real distances/drive times to candidate hotels.

Bottom line: the starred list, addresses and cuisine are verified. Prices, hours, social links, distances, and current operating status are NOT yet confirmed and should be treated as unverified until sourced.
