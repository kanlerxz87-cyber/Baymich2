Questions & answers

Items to address before decisions, plus suggestions for discovering missing information. No manual input — everything must come from official sources.

Q1. Which listed-only (no-star) restaurants should be included?
A: The guide lists 116 listed-no-star restaurants (30 Bib Gourmand + 86 Selected). Only a page-1 subset (22) is currently enumerated. Next: fetch MICHELIN index pages 2–4 and each restaurant's official detail page to build the full listed-only table.
Q2. What is the real price for 2 adults at each restaurant?
A: MICHELIN does not publish dollar totals. All priceFor2Adults values are blank/unverified. Next: check each restaurant's official menu and sum a representative two-person meal; leave blank unless confirmed from the official site.
Q3. Are there official YouTube / TikTok links?
A: None found on official sources for any restaurant; all left blank/unverified. Instagram verified for 28 of 29 starred restaurants. Next: search each restaurant's official website and social profiles for YouTube/TikTok; confirm Compis and Madcap Instagram too.
Q4. Are hours/phones complete?
A: Mostly yes for starred restaurants, but Commis, Saison, Sons & Daughters, Birdsong hours and Nisei/Sun Moon Studio/Restaurant Naides phones were not fully captured and are flagged unverified. Next: re-fetch those official MICHELIN detail pages (higher chunk indices) or the restaurant's own site.
Q5. How are hotels ranked, and is it trustworthy?
A: Not yet trustworthy — it's a neighbourhood-cluster proxy, not verified geocoding. Hotel addresses/distances/counts are blank/unverified. Next: geocode each hotel and each starred restaurant, compute true distances, and re-rank. Add Oakland/San Mateo/Marin hotels.
Q6. What about Napa/Sonoma starred venues (French Laundry, SingleThread)?
A: Excluded — they're in MICHELIN's separate "Napa / Sonoma Valleys" area, not the "San Francisco and surroundings" view used here. Decision needed: include them as a separate "Extended Bay Area" section, or keep scope to SF/surroundings.
Q7. How to keep data current?
A: MICHELIN stars change annually (ceremony ~June). Add a "last verified" date and re-verify against guide.michelin.com after each ceremony. Next: set a reminder to re-verify at the next MICHELIN California ceremony.
Q8. How to handle unverified values?
A: Per policy, leave blank and flag unverified; never infer. Show a "Verified / Unverified" filter so users can choose what to trust.
