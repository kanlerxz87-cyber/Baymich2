Executive summary

  • 29 starred restaurants in the official Michelin San Francisco listing: 4 three-star, 7 two-star, 18 one-star (Michelin SF listing, read 2026-11-24).
  • 43 Bay Area Bib Gourmand restaurants on the official Michelin California 2026 list (San Francisco 12, Oakland 10).
  • New 2026 stars in the SF group: Californios, Restaurant Naides, Wolfsbane. Enclos (Sonoma) and Californios (San Francisco) were promoted to three stars in June 2026.
  • Michelin shows only a price tier ($–$$$$) for each restaurant. No dollar price for two has been verified yet, so the price column is blank on purpose.
  • Verification depth: 6 starred rows are complete (address, cuisine, tier, hours, website); 5 are partial; 18 have only listing data (name, tier, cuisine).
  • 12 additional Bay Area star claims come from press only and are held on the Flagged page until checked on michelin.com. One conflict (Selby's city) is open.
  • Social links (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube) and hotel rankings are not yet verified. See Suggestions and Questions.

Plain-English summary: the official guide is clear about which restaurants have stars and Bibs. It does not give dollar prices, and it does not link social accounts. Those gaps are open, and the Questions page lists what is needed to close them.
